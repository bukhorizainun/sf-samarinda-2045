The Game

A collaborative game, not a race to answer first

Five players hold different roles and move through six phases together. You discuss, weigh other parties' interests, manage resources, absorb unexpected events, and settle on shared action.

  1. 01Observe the Present

    A system map: understanding the city and how its problems interlock.

    15–18 min

  2. 02Imagine Futures

    Three scenarios: Expected, Alternative, and Transformative Future.

    15–18 min

  3. 03Choose a Future

    One scenario is chosen. It passes with at least 4 of 5 roles behind it and no unaddressed critical harm.

    15–18 min

  4. 04Make Decisions

    Actions are chosen, risks weighed, and impacts simulated.

    15–18 min

  5. 05Act Together

    Three projects reach Committed status, with their network of backers mapped.

    15–18 min

  6. 06Real Impact

    A simulated impact profile, then one real commitment outside the game.

    15–18 min
