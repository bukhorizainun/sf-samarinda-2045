Privacy

What this site keeps, and what it does not

This page is short and plain. It is not a general promise; it describes what the site actually does when you open it.

In shortfour lines
  • 01No accounts and no forms.
  • 02No analytics and no advertising cookies.
  • 03The conversation and the score are computed in your browser.
  • 04The only stored thing is your light or dark preference.

01 / 02The detail

  • ✓

    Your theme choice

    If you switch between light and dark, that choice is stored in your own browser under the key sf-theme. It is never sent anywhere, and it disappears when you clear site data.

  • ✓

    The Shelbot conversation

    Shelbot runs entirely in your browser. Its answers come from the site text, the game guide, and the 184 cards that ship with the page. No question is sent to a server, and the conversation is gone once you close the page.

  • ✓

    Your mini game score

    The score is counted in your browser and never stored. The share button only acts when you press it: it hands one sentence with your score and the page address to your device's share sheet, or copies it to your clipboard.

  • ✕

    No analytics

    There is no Google Analytics, no tracking pixel, and no advertising cookie. This site does not follow where you go after you close the page.

  • ✕

    No third-party requests

    The Fraunces and Inter typefaces are served from this site's own server, not from Google. Illustrations are drawn as SVG inside the page. Opening this page therefore does not tell any third party that you opened it.

  • ✕

    No sign-up form

    There is no account, no name field, and no email field on this site. Getting in touch runs through WhatsApp or email, both of which open in your own app.

  • !

    What we do not control

    The site is served by a hosting provider, and providers like that normally record IP addresses and page requests in their own server logs. Those logs belong to the provider, not to us, and we do not read them.

02 / 02Going forward

If this changes

The site is still growing. If the AI companion later moves to a server so it can answer more widely, the questions you send will leave your browser, and this page will be updated before that change is switched on.

Asking about this

Questions about this page can go to the email below. Because no visitor data is stored, there is nothing to request back or to delete.
